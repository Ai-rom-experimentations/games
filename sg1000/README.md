# SG-1000

AI assisted, non-ported homebrew titles for the Sega SG-1000
(Z80 + TMS9918A + SN76489).

| title | folder | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Pong** | [`pong/`](pong) | a close reconstruction of Atari's 1972 arcade Pong - Z80 assembly, 16 KB cartridge, 1 or 2 players ([screenshots](pong/screenshots), [README](pong/README.md)) |
| **DELTARUNE Chapter 3 Hidden Racing Minigame** | [`deltarune-race/`](deltarune-race) | the racing game hidden behind Kris' back in Deltarune Chapter 3: drive a white square clockwise round a black track with a white outline and a white square in the middle, three laps against Susie, with her portrait and her line in a dialogue box - and, behind button 1+2, a secret race against the Roaring Knight ([screenshots](deltarune-race/screenshots), [README](deltarune-race/README.md)) |
